## GitHub
The GitHub repository for the Create: Terraworks modpack on Modrinth.\
This repo contains the files from the .mrpack file, and can additionally be used for issue reporting.\
You can find the modpack here: https://modrinth.com/modpack/create-terraworks

# Welcome!

This modpack expands Minecraft while keeping vanilla and the Create mod style as its foundation. Explore a vast world, build insane Create machines, discover new and revamped structures, and make your builds look better than ever!

I wanted to combine some of my favorite mods into one pack that feels like a natural evolution of Minecraft and Create while adding TONS of content — more items, a more exciting world, and a bunch of new ways to build.

# Features:

## ⚙️ Create and a ton of addons

Create is the main focus of the pack, with a bunch of addons that expand what you can build and automate.

![Blaze Cake Farm](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/f9833fb19605aa13714c44925a880f9cb144e6f9.jpeg)

Some included addons:

- Create: Aeronautics
- Create: New Age
- Copycats+
- Slice & Dice
- Central Kitchen
- Enchantment Industry
- Create: Connected
- Numismatics
- And wayyyy more!

Whether you want to build a giant factory, automate everything you can think of, or just make some cool looking machines, there's plenty to mess around with.

## 🌎 A bigger world, without losing the vanilla feeling

Using **Terralith** and **Tectonic**, the overworld has been completely improved with massive landscapes, new terrain, and more interesting places to explore while still feeling like Minecraft.

![Mountain Range](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/8584103acd69d677c33d0a5c3588ae561999c809_350.webp)
![Mountain](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/f952ff9dba15b3aa690804297b442203f98e323e_350.webp)

The Nether has been expanded with **Amplified Nether**, and the End has received a huge refresh with **Enderscape**, making both dimensions feel much more exciting to explore.

![The Nether](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/9096c60a1618e50671cfd7a3e2bd0aeb00dceba1_350.webp)
![The End](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/7b88f8ec559ed7184a2039b9b7f9eef7a4595f0d_350.webp)

## 🏰 More structures and things to discover

The world is filled with new structures, ranging from small vanilla-style additions to massive locations and even new bosses.

![Fortified Village](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/9eee9478365e2e8bf6e14543937e5b44e1e8d287_350.webp)
![Lich Tower](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/2be0fb8d555f79fcb0adbe8643722164b2315d79_350.webp)

Vanilla structures have also been improved, so exploring feels brand new again!

**Playing with friends?** Loot is player-specific, meaning everyone gets their own rewards instead of fighting over the same chest. Your backpack will also help you carry all the stuff you'll find on your adventures.

## ✨ Accessories

You will find new accessories that can be equipped outside of your usual 4 armor slots.

![Inventory](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/9f5fc3759d6826cef479d22ce3fa8cd3a5468537.jpeg)

Each one has its own unique ability, giving you new ways to play and customize your character.

## 🧱 Building and decoration

The Universal Chisel lets you turn almost any block into TONS and TONS of different variants, giving builders way more options.

![Universal Chisel GUI](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/qYdT4zyi/images/9de46aa231aba4c76be9eb75a84e9c12693bce9b.jpeg)

There are also:

- Simple vanilla-style furniture
- Lots of Create decorations
- Extra building blocks
- Plenty of small additions to make your builds stand out

## Requirements

⚠️ This modpack is pretty heavy and requires a decent PC.

It is recommended to allocate at least 8 GiB of RAM.
